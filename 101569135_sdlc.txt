Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) - Introduction

The software development lifecycle is the process teams follow to take an idea
to working software. The usual stages are planning, requirements, design,
implementation, testing, deployment and maintenance. Different teams organize
these stages in different ways (waterfall, agile and so on), but the stages
themselves stay mostly the same. A clear lifecycle keeps a team organized and
helps catch problems before they get expensive.

Testing and Early Feedback

Testing checks that software does what it should and helps find bugs before
users do. It can happen at several levels, from small unit tests to full
system tests. The earlier a problem is found, the cheaper it is to fix.
Early feedback from teammates, reviewers and users keeps the project moving in
the right direction and avoids building the wrong thing. Automated tests also
make it safer to change code later.
