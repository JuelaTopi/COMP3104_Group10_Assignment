Importance of DevOps in the Software Development Lifecycle

The software development lifecycle includes planning, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
DevOps helps connect these stages by encouraging teams to share responsibility and automate repetitive work.
Frequent feedback helps teams identify problems earlier and improve their software.

Feedback and Reliability

During development, version control keeps a history of changes and supports collaboration.
During testing, automated checks can identify issues before changes are merged.
During deployment, automation can make release steps more consistent.
During maintenance, monitoring and user feedback help teams identify improvements.

Small Changes and Shared Responsibility

Small, clear commits make changes easier to review and troubleshoot.
Developers and operations teams communicate throughout the lifecycle instead of working separately.
DevOps can reduce delays, but reliable software still depends on useful tests, careful review, and good communication.
