George Brown College - Introduction

My name is Arcadio Vega, but everyone calls me Archie. I study at George Brown
College in Toronto, in the Computer Programming and Analysis program (T197).
George Brown is a polytechnic college that focuses on hands-on, career-ready
education. It works closely with industry, which is a big reason I chose it.
I expect to graduate in April 2027.

My Program and Learning Experience

Before studying programming I spent about 15 years in professional kitchens,
most recently as a Chef de Cuisine in Toronto. I am changing careers into
software development. The program has taught me Java, databases, web
development and how to work in teams. My classes are online, and I work evening
shifts, so I have learned to manage my time carefully. I have made the Dean's
List and I like building real projects, including small apps for my restaurant.
Cooking taught me to work under pressure and communicate clearly, and those
skills carry over to working on a development team.
